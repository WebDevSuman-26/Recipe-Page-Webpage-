🥞 Masala Dosa

Quick Facts

Prep Time10 hrs
Cook Time20 mins
Servings4

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Urad Dal
  • Potatoes
  • Onions

Steps

  1. Soak and grind batter.
  2. Ferment overnight.
  3. Cook on tawa.
  4. Add filling and fold.

🍘 Idli

Quick Facts

Prep Time10 hrs
Cook Time15 mins
Servings4

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Urad Dal
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Prepare batter.
  2. Ferment overnight.
  3. Steam in molds.

🍗 Butter Chicken

Quick Facts

Prep Time30 mins
Cook Time40 mins
Servings4

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Butter
  • Tomato puree
  • Cream

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken.
  2. Cook and prepare gravy.
  3. Mix and serve.

🥘 Chole Bhature

Quick Facts

Prep Time8 hrs
Cook Time30 mins
Servings4

  • Chickpeas

  • Flour
